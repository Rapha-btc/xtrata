# XIP-000: Xtrata Improvement Proposal Process

## Abstract

Xtrata Improvement Proposals (XIPs) are design documents that describe standards, conventions, interfaces, formats and processes used within the Xtrata ecosystem.

## Motivation

XIPs provide a mechanism for documenting and evolving standards used by wallets, marketplaces, explorers, indexers and applications built on Xtrata.

## Categories

### Standards Track
Defines interoperable formats and behaviours.

### Informational
Guidelines and recommendations.

### Process
Governance and procedural standards.

## Lifecycle

Draft -> Review -> Accepted -> Implemented -> Deprecated -> Superseded

## Numbering

XIPs are assigned sequential identifiers and numbers are never reused.

## Principles

- Permanence
- Simplicity
- Composability
- Verifiability
- Decentralisation

## Relationship to Xtrata

The Xtrata contract preserves data and provenance.

XIPs preserve interoperability.
