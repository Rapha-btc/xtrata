About this transition

Xboard is a transitional display of the original StacksBoard. StacksBoard was a pioneering OG experiment, but like many early on-chain projects its live presentation still relies on too much Web2 infrastructure.

This version keeps the original StacksBoard holder map visible while testing the next step: a more on-chain board where display content comes from Stacks-chain inscriptions rather than loose external media links.

Who made this?

Xboard is an independent holder-made experiment. I am not the creator of the original StacksBoard project. I am a holder of real estate on the original board, and this is my attempt to help make that real estate useful and visible again.
