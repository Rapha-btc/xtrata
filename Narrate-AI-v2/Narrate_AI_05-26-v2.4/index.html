🎨 Voice Design Lab
Browse the full preset voice library, create designed voices, then audition each one with a short sample.
All voice presets ›
Each card starts with a distinct voice-design prompt, preview line, and sample script. Tweak any wording before creating the voice. Once created, you can audition it immediately and optionally set it as the active synthesis voice.
Voice design creates a new reusable custom voice on your DashScope account using Narrate:AI Design. The sample button then generates fresh speech through that designed voice so you can test it on a slightly longer read. The language dropdown sets the voice's language code. Accent and regional identity still come from the voice prompt itself, so prompts that need British, Australian, or other non-US English should say that explicitly. The new French, Spanish, and German cards are designed to be created in their native language first, then checked with an English sample to see how much accent identity carries across.

📖 Audiobook Workflow
Flash is the main engine, Director is the selective polish pass, and chunk review stays in one place.
Long-form mode ›

Paste a full manuscript or script. The analyser detects chapter headings, preserves paragraph structure, assigns narrator or character roles, then generates a chunk-based audiobook workflow where Narrate:AI Flash handles one-click throughput and Narrate:AI Director can be applied selectively where the performance needs more intention.

The Titles track is generated as one continuous chunk. Pause markers are read inside the same performance, and title narration automatically asks for slower speech and longer pauses where supported.
