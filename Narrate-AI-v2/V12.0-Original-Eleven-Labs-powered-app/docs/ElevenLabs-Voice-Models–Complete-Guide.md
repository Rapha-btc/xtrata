# 🎙️ ElevenLabs Voice Models – Complete Guide

This document explains the differences between the ElevenLabs models available in the app, and helps you choose the right one depending on your use case.

---

## 🧠 Overview

ElevenLabs models fall into three main categories:

| Category | Models | Purpose |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 🎭 **High Quality / Storytelling** | v3, Multilingual v2 | Realism, emotion, narrative |
| ⚡ **Fast / Real-Time** | Flash v2.5, Turbo v2.5 | Speed, responsiveness |
| 🧓 **Legacy** | Monolingual v1 | Older compatibility |

---

## 🎭 Eleven v3 (Latest & Most Advanced)

### Description
Eleven v3 is the newest and most advanced model, designed for **expressive storytelling and character-driven audio**.

### Key Features
- Highly realistic speech
- Advanced emotional range
- Natural pacing and delivery
- Multi-speaker and dialogue support
- Context-aware tone shifts

### Quality
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Best available
- Most human-like output
- Ideal for cinematic or immersive experiences

### Language Support
- 70+ languages
- Strong cross-language consistency

### Limitations
- Slower generation speed
- Not suitable for real-time use
- Requires more careful text formatting

### Recommended Use Cases
- Audiobooks
- Narrative storytelling
- Character dialogue (e.g. dual voice mode)
- High-end creative content

---

## 🎭 Multilingual v2 (Stable & Production-Ready)

### Description
Multilingual v2 is the **most reliable and widely used model** for production environments.

### Key Features
- Consistent voice quality
- Natural tone and pacing
- Strong long-form performance

### Quality
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ High quality and stable
- Slightly less expressive than v3

### Language Support
- ~29 languages
- Good accent and pronunciation consistency

### Limitations
- Less emotional range than v3
- Slower than real-time models

### Recommended Use Cases
- Voiceovers
- Podcasts
- YouTube narration
- Production pipelines requiring consistency

---

## ⚡ Flash v2.5 (Ultra Fast)

### Description
Flash v2.5 is the **fastest model available**, designed for real-time applications.

### Key Features
- Extremely low latency (~75ms)
- Cost-efficient
- Scales well for high-volume usage

### Quality
- ⭐⭐⭐☆☆ Good, but less expressive
- Prioritises speed over nuance

### Language Support
- ~32 languages

### Limitations
- Flatter emotional delivery
- Less precise handling of complex text

### Recommended Use Cases
- Live voice agents
- Interactive apps
- Games
- Real-time AI systems

---

## ⚡ Turbo v2.5 (Fast, Legacy Real-Time)

### Description
Turbo v2.5 is a fast model similar to Flash, but now largely superseded.

### Key Features
- Faster than Multilingual v2
- Moderate latency (~300–400ms)

### Quality
- ⭐⭐⭐☆☆ Comparable to Multilingual v2 in some cases
- Slightly less natural in delivery

### Language Support
- ~32 languages

### Limitations
- Slower than Flash
- Less expressive than newer models

### Recommended Use Cases
- Older real-time pipelines
- Compatibility scenarios

---

## 🧓 Monolingual v1 (Legacy)

### Description
The original English-only model.

### Quality
- ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Outdated
- Lower realism compared to modern models

### Language Support
- English only

### Recommended Use Cases
- Legacy systems only
- Not recommended for new projects

## 💡 Pro Tips

- The **model is only part of the result**:
  - Voice selection matters just as much
  - Text formatting significantly affects output
  - Dialogue structure improves realism

- For dual voice setups (e.g. Sierra / Finn):
  - Alternate lines clearly
  - Keep sentences natural and conversational
  - Avoid overly long paragraphs
