## Hackathons and builder programs report

**Topic:** Opportunities for a Stacks-based inscription protocol, app, or platform
**Date:** April 5, 2026

### Executive summary

The opportunity set breaks into three clear buckets. First, there are **direct Stacks-native paths** such as the Stacks Endowment grants and the emerging Ascent pipeline, which are the most strategically aligned for an inscription protocol positioned as core Bitcoin app infrastructure. Second, there are **high-fit adjacent programs** focused on agentic payments, AI x crypto, permanent data, and on-chain application backends, where Xtrata-style immutable data inscriptions can be framed as durable state, provenance, memory, publishing, or backend infrastructure. Third, there are **venture-style accelerators** that are not chain-specific but are highly relevant if the goal is to package the project as a broader startup rather than only an ecosystem tool. ([stacks.co][1])

### Strongest immediate targets

The best near-term live or near-live opportunities appear to be **Stellar Hacks: Agents**, which is running from March 30 to April 13, 2026 and is explicitly focused on agents, AI, x402, and programmable payments; **Avalanche Retro9000 C-Chain Round 2**, which is open with an April 16, 2026 deadline; **Alliance**, which is positioned as a rolling crypto-and-AI accelerator with fast review; and **Filecoin Foundation Grants**, which remain a live route for developer tooling, integrations, applications, and research. These are especially relevant if the pitch emphasizes agent memory, API receipts, creator publishing, durable media, or verifiable application state. ([DoraHacks][2])

### Best Stacks-native routes

For Stacks itself, the clearest top-level path is the **Stacks Endowment grant program**, which in its 2026 cycle offered Builder Grants up to $50,000 and Getting Started Grants up to $10,000, though that latest window closed on **March 13, 2026**. Even though that deadline has passed, it is still highly important because it shows Stacks now has a more formal funding ladder for serious builders. Alongside that, **Stacks Ascent** appears to be the main structured builder-growth route, and Stacks’ 2025/2026 ecosystem materials indicate that Ascent, DeGrants, hackathons, and Rise In onboarding are becoming part of a connected pipeline rather than isolated programs. ([stacks.co][1])

### Best adjacent ecosystems for Xtrata’s thesis

Outside Stacks, the strongest thematic fits are the ecosystems already funding **permanent data, developer tooling, trusted knowledge, AI coordination, and machine payments**. Filecoin is a standout because it is actively funding core infra and tooling, including through ProPGF. Arweave remains relevant as the closest permanent-data comparison set. XRPL is expanding funding routes through grants and accelerator-style support. Arbitrum Open House and Base Batches are also useful because they sit closer to application distribution and founder support, which matters if the protocol is framed not just as infra, but as a launchpad for on-chain apps and creator products. ([Filecoin Foundation][3])

### Best founder-scale accelerators

If the objective is to raise profile, sharpen positioning, and potentially secure capital, the strongest non-ecosystem-specific options are **Alliance** and **a16z CSX**, with Alliance especially relevant because it explicitly markets itself around crypto and AI. These are better fits if the story is “new application infrastructure for durable on-chain data and agentic systems,” rather than “just another chain-native tool.” ([Alliance][4])

### Practical takeaway

For Xtrata or a similar Stacks inscription platform, the most promising positioning is not only “Bitcoin/Stacks infra,” but **immutable backend for AI agents, creator systems, identity/data integrity, and truly on-chain apps**. That framing broadens the target list dramatically. The best immediate move would be to prioritize one **current application window** in the AI-agent or infra space, while preparing a stronger Stacks-native application package for the next Endowment / Ascent cycle. ([DoraHacks][2])
